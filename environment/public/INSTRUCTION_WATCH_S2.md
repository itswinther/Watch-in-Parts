# Task: stage 2 of 4, add a 24-hour hand

`work/` holds a finished watch movement designed by another engineer (a copy is
in `previous/`). Add a 24-hour hand to it, following "Stage 2" in
`SPEC_WATCH.md`. The existing mobiles are frozen; the spec says exactly what may
and may not change.

Write the result into `work/` and submit as the spec describes. Only the last
accepted submission is graded, by a separate program. You have about 35 minutes.

## How you are run

You run unattended in one session. Run everything in the foreground and do not
end your turn while a job of yours is still running: nothing will wake you up.
You are stopped 39 minutes after you start, whatever you are doing. Whatever is in
the submission folder at that moment is what gets graded, so submit a first
version early and resubmit when it improves.
