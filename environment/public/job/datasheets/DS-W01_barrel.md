# Mainspring barrels, demonstration-scale series: catalogue sheet

| Document | Revision | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| DS-W01 | A | 2026-11-14 | Released |

**Issued by:** the barrel supplier. **Document type:** catalogue sheet.

## 1. Scope

Going barrels with an integral toothed drum, for demonstration-scale movements. Each barrel is one rigid body:
the toothed drum and its arbor turn together. Teeth: involute spur teeth, addendum 1.0 x module, dedendum
1.25 x module. All lengths in millimetres.

## 3. BR-096-20 as supplied

The barrel BR-096-20 is supplied as a mesh in its assembled position, `/app/supplied/barrel.stl` (binary STL,
millimetres). In that position:

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Axis | the vertical line x = 0 mm, y = 0 mm |
| Toothed drum | z 0 mm to z 1 mm |
| Arbor | z -1 mm to z 1.6 mm |
| Tooth phase | one tooth centred on the +x direction (0 degrees from +x, measured on the supplied mesh) |
| Volume of the mesh | 288.9 mm3 |
