Mainspring wound:
Illustrative link. The crown-winding mechanism (keyless works) is designed and simulated separately and is not yet joined to this movement. Here the crown's effect is kinematic: pushed in, it turns the barrel arbor; pulled out, it turns the motion work and stops the going train. The crown ratios and the spring capacity are illustrative; the train and hand speeds are exact.
