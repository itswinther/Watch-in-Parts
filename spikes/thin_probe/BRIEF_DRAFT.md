# Thinness probe: brief draft for workstream B

Written 15 Nov by itswinther's brainstorm session (Claude), from itswinther's answers in that session. Not
reviewed: itswinther chose a quick draft with no review for the probe. Nothing here is in the task yet. The
decisions behind it are under "Proposed spec changes" in `SESSION.md` (entry of 15 Nov, "Thinness probe").

## What the probe is

One run each of Fable 5.2, Opus 5.5 and GPT-6.1-sol, locally (`environment/run_clean_job.sh` for Fable and
Opus, Codex for GPT), on a brief with two changes:

1. QA-W100 says what must hold, not how the rig tests it. Every number stays, restated as a requirement.
2. The order asks for a thin movement. QA-W100 states a thickness target of 9.0 mm and the score formula.

Unchanged: the grader, barrel BR-096-20, the reference, the 3 h agent limit. The thickness score is
computed afterwards from each run's details file (below), so the grader needs no change.

The question it answers: when asked for a thin movement, do frontier models break requirements or stop well
above 9 mm? Either one is a promising gap signal (itswinther). One known risk: 9.0 mm is unproven. The
session's rough floor for this barrel is about 8 mm (arbor z -1.0 to 5.6, held at both ends; every hand above
the highest fixed part), and the reference is 13.1 mm. Before the final task, an expert reference must reach
the stated target, or a judge can call the target a trick.

## Where the edits go

PROMPT.md, FORMAT.md and QA-W100 are generated by `environment/make_brief_job.py`, which also checks that every
graded number has exactly one home cell (`make_bundle_job.py` refuses the bundle otherwise). So:

- Make the edits on a probe branch, not on `main`, so the submitted task does not change.
- The new number 9.0 mm needs a constant (e.g. `THIN_TARGET` in `geometry/watch.py`) and a home cell in
  QA-W100's thickness section, registered in `TRACEABILITY_WATCH.md`. The formula cites the case height from
  DS-W02; it must not print 13.4.
- Or, faster: hand-edit the generated files on the probe branch and skip `make_brief_job.py --check`. Then
  `make_bundle_job.py` will fail its number check, and `run_clean_job.sh` must be pointed at a hand-built folder.
  That is B's call.

### Section 5, Part 1: conditions and requirements 1 to 13

> Conditions. If one fails, Part 1 fails and none of its requirements is met.
>
> - **C1 Files.** As rev A.
> - **C2 Assembled.** In the assembled positions, taking the frame as one solid and each mobile (the barrel
>   included) as one solid, no two of these solids collide; no two fixed parts collide; no two parts of one
>   mobile collide. The fixed parts form one frame: every fixed part is linked to every other through a chain
>   of fixed parts, each within 0.01 mm of the next.
>
> Requirements.
>
> 1. **Free to turn.** Each mobile, the barrel included, turns through a full turn about its axis without
>    colliding with the frame at any angle.
> 2. **Held.** No mobile, the barrel included, can move 0.3 mm on its own along +x, -x, +y, -y, +z or -z.
>    Moved that far in any of these directions, with everything else in place, it is stopped by the frame, or
>    by a mobile whose axis is within 0.01 mm of its own and which is itself held in that direction. A mobile on
>    another axis never holds it.
> 3. **Seated.** Every part of a mobile that has more than one part is fixed in its mobile. Moved 0.3 mm along
>    +x, -x, +y or -y, it is stopped by the other parts of its mobile, and moved 0.3 mm along +z or along -z (at
>    least one of the two) it is stopped too. The parts of each such mobile form one piece, each within 0.02 mm
>    of the next. The barrel and one-part mobiles are exempt.
> 4. **The train runs.** At least one mesh is declared. No mobile gets two different speeds from the declared
>    meshes, and every mobile is driven from the barrel through declared meshes that drive. Every declared mesh
>    drives. A declared mesh, D its `driver` (z_D teeth) and N its `driven` (z_N teeth), drives when, as D
>    turns N at the ratio z_D / z_N through a full turn of the larger gear:
>    - the two never collide;
>    - they stay in mesh: wherever they do not touch, they are at most 0.1 mm apart;
>    - the teeth carry the drive: from every position of that turn, turning D a further quarter of its tooth
>      pitch (90 / z_D degrees) while N is held makes D cut into N. The region cut into N is at least 0.001 mm3,
>      and its volume divided by its height along z is more than 0.5 x m x m, with m = 2 x (distance between
>      the two axes) / (z_D + z_N).
>
>    Every declared mesh is external: its two mobiles turn in opposite directions. A mesh that does not drive
>    drives nothing.
> 5. **Runs with its arbors slightly out of place.** Every declared mesh also drives with N's axis moved 0.03 mm
>    straight towards D's axis, and with it moved 0.03 mm straight away.
> 6. **Nothing else collides.** As the movement runs, at any time of day, no two mobiles that are not declared
>    as a mesh ever collide with each other.
> 7. **Hour hand.** 8. **Minute hand.** 9. **Seconds hand.** As rev A.
> 10. **Hours and minutes on a common centre.** As rev A.
> 11. **Hands.** Each of the hour, minute and seconds hand parts reaches at least 5 mm from its mobile's axis;
>     lies wholly above the frame (its lowest point is no lower than the highest point of any fixed part);
>     points (its centre of volume is at least 0.5 mm from its mobile's axis); and, seen from the dial side,
>     covers at most a quarter of the disc its tip sweeps.
> 12. **Fits the case.** As rev A.
> 13. **Printable.** As rev A.
> 14. **Gear strength.** Rev A, word for word.

### New section 9: Thickness

> ## 9. Thickness
>
> The order asks for a slim calibre. The movement's height H is the highest point minus the lowest point of
> every part of the movement and of the barrel, in the assembled positions, along the axis. The taller of
> `movement.json` and `variant_6h.json` counts. The target is **9.0 mm**.
>
> With C the inner height of the case named in the work order (DS-W02, section 2, column "Inner height along
> the axis (mm)"):
>
>     x = (H - 9.0) / (C - 9.0), taken as 0 below 0 and as 1 above 1
>     thickness score s = (1 - x) ^ 4
>
> The acceptance figure is 1/2 x (the result of Parts 1 to 4 together) + 1/2 x [every condition and every
> requirement of Parts 1 to 4 is met] x s, times the statement factor of section 3. A movement file that does
> not load counts as no thickness score (s = 0).

That is the formula only, with no worked table (itswinther's choice).

## 5. Scoring a probe run afterwards

From the grader's details file (`--details D`), with no grader change:

```python
import json, sys
d = json.load(open(sys.argv[1]))          # the details file of one run
C, T = 13.4, 9.0                           # case inner height (DS-W02 CS-414-134), thickness target
hs = [p["details"]["size"]["z_max"] - p["details"]["size"]["z_min"]
      for p in (d["parts"]["P1"], d["parts"]["P3"]) if "z_max" in p["details"].get("size", {})]
H = max(hs) if len(hs) == 2 else None      # both builds must load
x = 1.0 if H is None else min(1.0, max(0.0, (H - T) / (C - T)))
s = (1.0 - x) ** 4
A = 1.0 if d["all_pass"] else 0.0
R = d["parts_reward"] - 0.5 * A + 0.5 * A * s
print("H %s  s %.3f  reward %.4f" % (H, s, R * d["honesty"]["factor"]))
```

For the delivered reference (H 13.1 mm, all pass), this gives s = 0.00002 and reward 0.50: the old reference
is a thick design under the new rule.

## 6. What to collect from each run (itswinther's list)

| What | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Height H, and which requirements failed | the details file: `parts.P1/P3.details.size`, every `checks` entry |
| Time used against the 3 h limit, and whether the agent stopped on its own | the run log; a run that hit the limit is not gap evidence |
| Where and why it stopped thinning | the transcript: the layout it chose, what it tried, why it stopped (satisfied, blocked by a requirement, gave up) |
| Its own claimed height | `report.json` `height_mm`, next to the measured H |

Also worth noting by hand, since the probe has no minimum-wall rule: the thinnest plate and bridge in each
delivery. A low H made of 0.2 mm plates is not the gap we want to claim.
